# Sources of the named trophies

[Back to the README](../README.md) · English

What each named trophy was measured on (see [likeness.md](likeness.md)). A photograph is only looked at and measured: it is never copied into the package, so no licence text for it is kept in `LICENSES/`. Its licence is recorded here so that the status of the reference is known. "Approximate" marks a drawing made from a description, or from a photograph too small to measure closely.

| Emblem | Trophy | Reference looked at | Licence of the reference | Approximate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `trophy:paterson-cup` | Paterson Cup | A 1912 studio photograph of the cup on its base, ribbon at the foot (hockeygods.com, image 10889, 341 by 480 pixels) | Not ours; only looked at and measured | The base's stages and the bowl's lower curve are read from a small photograph, and the ribbon is not drawn |
