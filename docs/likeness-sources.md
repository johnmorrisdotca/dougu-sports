[Back to the README](../README.md) · English

A photograph is only looked at and measured: it is never copied into the package, so no licence text for it is kept in `LICENSES/`. Its licence is recorded here so that the status of the reference is known. "Approximate" marks a drawing made from a description, or from a photograph too small to measure closely.
