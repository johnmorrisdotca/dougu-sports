The icons of [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) are under this licence, each by its own author. The full legal code is at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode>, and the summary a reader can follow is at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.

What the licence asks of this package, and what it does:

- **Credit the author**: every emblem made from a game-icons.net icon has a row in [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) naming the icon, its
  author and its page on game-icons.net, and the README and the demo point to that file.
- **Link to the licence**: the row names the licence and links to it.
- **Say that the icon was changed**: the row says how it was changed (recoloured to the three styles, scaled, its path simplified).

No emblem uses a game-icons.net icon yet. The legal code is linked and not copied here: the text a reader needs to check is the one
the Creative Commons publishes.
