# Notice: where the art comes from

The code of this package is under the MIT licence (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [LICENSES/MIT.txt](LICENSES/MIT.txt)). Every emblem is either drawn for this package (`source: "ours"` in `emblemInfo`) or made from one of the sources below, and each emblem made from a source has a row in the second table. The licence text of each source is in [LICENSES/](LICENSES/).

No emblem carries the name, mark or logo of a league, a team, a federation or an event: the trophies, crests and balls are descriptive and generic.
